Questions

Common questions.

Do I need a finance or sales background?

No. You get training on the products and on how to have simple, honest conversations with families. Many good advisors started with no sales experience.

Is there any cost to join?

There is a small one-time fee for the licensing exam and registration. I'll share the exact amount on our call, before you commit to anything.

How much can I earn?

It depends on how many families you help. There is no fixed salary. We go through how earnings work, with real examples, on the call.

Can I do this part-time?

Yes. Many advisors start with a few hours a week alongside home, studies or another job.

Do I need to go to an office?

No. Most work happens on phone, WhatsApp and short meetings with families near you.

Who can apply?

Anyone 18 or older living in who has passed 10th or 12th and is comfortable talking to people. Final selection follows the insurer's process.
